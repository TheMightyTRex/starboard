# Finish what is still to set up

After you install your apps, Starboard lists what is left to do, such as adding a site to search or finishing
Jellyfin's set-up. **Go through them** takes you through the list one item at a time.

## Before you begin

- You installed apps with Starboard. Read [Install apps with Starboard](install-apps.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Home**, then **Dashboard**, or to **System**, then **Apps**. **Still to set up** lists each item, with
   why it matters.
2. Select **Go through them**. A side panel called **Still to set up** opens at the first item.
3. For each item, choose one of these:
   - **Do it now**: opens the place that does it. The panel carries on there.
   - **Done**: for a guided step in another app, such as Tdarr, Wizarr, Dispatcharr or Soulseek. Follow the
     step in that app, then select **Done**, because Starboard cannot tell when it is finished.
   - **Next step** or **Skip**: leaves it on the list for later and goes to the next item. On the last item,
     **Next step** is called **Finish**.
   - **Ignore**: takes it off the list, and Starboard does not ask about it again.
4. At the end, the panel says **That is all for now**. Select **Close**.

You can also select an item's name in the list to go straight to the place that does it.

## Things to note

- Each item goes from the list by itself when Starboard finds it done. The list goes when everything is done.
- The panel opens only when you select **Go through them**. If you leave it with **Open as a page**, it carries on
  where you were when you come back to the list.
- To get ignored items back, select **Bring back** and the number **ignored** on the Apps page.
- The items depend on your install. They can include **Add a site to search**, **Connect a download app**,
  **Add a TMDB key**, **Add your Usenet provider's server**, **Finish Jellyfin's set-up** and
  **Connect Reelix to your media servers**.
