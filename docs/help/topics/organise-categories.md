# Organise apps into categories

Categories are the headings apps sit under on the Apps page, such as "Watch, listen and request". You can
add, rename, reorder and remove categories, and choose the category for each app. Changes apply at once.

## Before you begin

- You have added at least one app. Read [Add an app](add-an-app.md).

## Steps

### Add, rename or reorder categories

1. Go to **Settings**, then **General**. The **Categories** section lists them in the order the Apps page shows
   them, with how many apps are in each.
2. To add one, type its name in **New category**, then select **Add category**.
3. To rename one, select **Rename** for that category. Type the **New name**, then select **Rename category**.
   The apps in it move with it.
4. To change the order, select the **More actions** button (three dots) for a category, then **Move up** or
   **Move down**.

### Remove a category

1. Select the **More actions** button for the category, then **Remove**.
2. If it has apps in it, choose where they go in **Move its apps to**.
3. Select **Remove category**. The apps themselves are not changed.

### Put an app in a category

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Manage apps**, and select **Edit** for the app.
2. In **Category**, choose a category. **Choose for me** puts well-known apps in their usual category and shows
   which one it picked.
3. Select **Save app**, or **Save and continue** for Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr and Readarr.

## Things to note

- Starboard starts with four categories: Watch, listen and request; Find and organise; Search and download;
  and Dashboards and tools.
- You can have up to 20 categories, with names of up to 40 characters. The last category cannot be removed.
- Apps with no category are shown under **Other**. Background services are listed under **Apps**, **Other
  services** instead. Read [Add a background service](add-a-background-service.md).
- Some apps may be in a category that is not in the list, for example after a restore. Starboard then
  offers **Add it to the list** or **Add them to the list**.
