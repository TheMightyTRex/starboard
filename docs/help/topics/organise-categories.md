# Organise apps into categories

Categories are the headings apps sit under on the Apps page, such as 'Watch, listen and request'. You can add,
rename, reorder and remove them, and choose each app's category. Changes apply at once.

## Before you begin

- You have added at least one app. Read [Add an app](add-an-app.md).

## Steps

### Add, rename or reorder categories

1. Go to **Settings**, then **General**.
2. Activate **Advanced settings**. The **Categories** section lists the categories in the order the Apps page
   shows them, with how many apps are in each.
3. To add one, enter its name in **New category**, then select **Add category**.
4. To rename one, select **Rename** for it. Enter the **New name**, then select **Rename category**. Its apps
   move with it.
5. To change the order, select **More actions** (three dots) for a category, then **Move up** or **Move down**.

### Remove a category

1. In **Categories**, select **More actions** for the category, then **Remove**.
2. If it has apps in it, choose where they go in **Move its apps to**.
3. Select **Remove category**. The apps themselves are not changed.

### Put an app in a category

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Apps**, then **Manage apps**, and select **Edit** for the app.
2. In **Category**, choose a category. **Choose for me** puts well-known apps in their usual category, and
   shows which one it picked.
3. Select **Save app**, or **Save and continue** for Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr and Readarr.

## Things to note

- Starboard starts with four categories: 'Watch, listen and request', 'Find and organise', 'Search and download'
  and 'Dashboards and tools'.
- You can have up to 20 categories, with names of up to 40 characters. The last category cannot be removed.
- Apps with no category are shown under 'Other'. Background services are listed on **System**, **Apps**,
  **Other services** instead. Read [Add a background service](add-a-background-service.md).
- After a restore, some apps may be in a category that is not in the list. Starboard then offers **Add it to
  the list** or **Add them to the list**.
- **Advanced settings** stays as you left it on every Settings page.
