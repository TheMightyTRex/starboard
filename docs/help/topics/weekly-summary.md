# Get a weekly summary

Starboard can send you a short message every Monday morning that says how the week went: how each app did, the
health of your disks and how full your folders are. It goes to the same place as your alerts.

## Before you begin

- Alerts are set up, with **Watch in the background** on and somewhere for alerts to go. Read
  [Turn on alerts](turn-on-alerts.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Alerts**.
2. Under **Weekly summary**, switch on **Weekly summary**. Starboard shows **Weekly summary on**.

The first summary comes on the next Monday, after 09:00 by the server's clock.

## What the summary says

- **Apps:** how many apps answered all week, and for each one that did not, how often it was not answering.
- **Disks:** how many drives are healthy, and which ones to check. This needs Disk health set up. Read
  [Check your disks' health](check-disk-health.md).
- **Folders:** how full each folder on System, Storage is. Read
  [Check free space and what is using it](check-storage.md).

## Things to note

- The summary uses the uptime history that watching keeps, so a new install has little to say in its first
  week.
- It is sent once a week. It costs nothing noticeable.
- The **Everything** preset on **Settings**, then **Performance**, turns the weekly summary on. Read
  [Make Starboard use less of your server](make-starboard-lighter.md).

## If it does not work

- **No summary on Monday:** check **Watch in the background** is on in **Settings**, then **Alerts**, and that
  **Send a test alert** reaches you.
- **The summary says no history yet:** watching has not been on long enough. Wait a week.
- If a summary cannot be sent, Starboard writes the reason in its log. Read
  [Read the log and report a problem](read-the-log.md).
