# Styles: Settings
<!-- Mirrored from the starboard-plugins repository by tools/sync-plugin-help.py: change it there. -->

Styles has no settings of its own, and no buttons. You choose one of its 22 styles in **Display**: select **Display** in the header, then choose under **Style**.

## Things to note

- **Style** shows in the Display panel only while Styles, or another plug-in with styles, is active.
- Your choice of style is kept in the browser you use, so each phone, computer and browser has its own. Choose it again on each one.
- **High contrast**, text size, wider spacing, underlined links and reduced motion in **Display** always win over a style.
- Six styles change every part of Starboard: cards, buttons, tabs, the header, the side menu and chips. They are Studio, Expressive, Swiss, Neon, Clay and Mica.
- A style changes colours, corner shapes, the font (from fonts already on your device), shadows, the page's gradient, a light texture and glass panels. It cannot change anything else.
- To go back to the standard style, select **Reset style** under the list of styles in **Display**.
