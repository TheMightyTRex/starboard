# Starboard v0.7.7

Released 4 November 2026.

## Changed

- **Pages open faster.** A page and its files now share one connection, and the browser keeps the styles, scripts and icons until they change. Opening a page takes less than half the server's processor time, and a page you have opened before about a sixth.
- **Live TV uses far less memory.** TV guides are read as they unpack instead of all at once. A large guide peaked at 197 MB and now takes 36 MB.
- **Starting up.** Starboard's code is prepared when the image is built, so it starts in about half the time.

## Fixed

- **Samsung TV Plus and Pluto TV guides.** They were refused because of a line at their start, and every stream was tested again every hour as a result. They now load, and the hourly check finds nothing to do.
- **Failed guides.** A guide that fails is tried again after 12 hours, not every hour.

## Security

- **Error answers.** Python's own error answers, such as for a request method Starboard does not handle, now carry the same security headers as every other answer (found by OWASP ZAP).
- **Checks.** Trivy, OWASP ZAP (signed out, and an active scan signed in), Gitleaks, Bandit, ShellCheck, Hadolint and CodeQL ran on this release. ZAP's two signed-in warnings were false alarms, explained in docs/SECURITY-AND-TESTS.md.

## Your settings and the way back

Nothing to choose, and no setting changes. Live TV builds its channels and guides once after the update. To go back, run ./update.sh --to v0.7.6.
