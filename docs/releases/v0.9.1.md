# Starboard v0.9.1

Released 5 November 2026.

## Fixed

- **Updating after a long gap.** ./update.sh stopped after listing what changed when more than 30 changes had come since your version. It now lists 30 and says how many more.

## Security

- **Checks.** Trivy, OWASP ZAP (signed out), Gitleaks, Bandit, ShellCheck, Hadolint and CodeQL ran on this release; only update.sh and the help changed since 0.9.0's full checks.

## Your settings and the way back

Nothing to choose. Updating from 0.9.0 to 0.9.1 with ./update.sh works as before. To go back, run ./update.sh --to v0.9.0.
