# Starboard v0.20.2

Released 17 November 2026.

## Fixed

- **Update now could stop at the backup.** Update now could stop with "data/self-update.log.new: No such file or directory" while it backed up your settings, because the page's own progress log was being rewritten beside them. The log is now written outside your settings folder, and a backup that meets a file changing is tried again.

## Your settings and the way back

Your settings stay as they are. To go back, run ./update.sh --to v0.20.1.
