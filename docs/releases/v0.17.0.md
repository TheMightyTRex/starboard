# Starboard v0.17.0

Released 14 November 2026.

## Added

- **Starboard's API 1.1.0.** The API now reads everything the pages show (Coming up, requests, speeds, download hours, history, the Library and its search, uptime, app updates, containers and more), and a second right, Read and manage, covers the everyday actions: approve or decline a request, pause or resume downloads, remove or block a download, build a Library tab again, look for container updates, run a Jellyfin job and send a test alert. /api/v1/events streams app changes and alerts as they happen. Make a key asks what each key may do and what it may see.
- **Developer help and AI assistants.** The help has a Developers section with the plug-in and API references (the API reference built from the API description), a quick start, a page listing what the API covers for every action on the pages, and Connect an AI assistant, which gives an assistant its own Read only key with the fewest scopes. /api/v1/logs gives Starboard's log with addresses, names and keys taken out, only to keys with Logs ticked.
- **App plug-ins.** A whole app can now come as a plug-in, kept apart from the main install, such as Jellystat with its database: it gets its own page on your home network at a port Starboard chooses, its own passwords made once for your server, and Remove asks where its folder goes. A plug-in can also copy an app you already run, such as Tdarr, into itself, without removing the old one. The Catalogue has a group, Apps you can add.
- **Plug-ins you can trust and update.** Adding a plug-in first shows what it can see, change and reach, with Install and Cancel; its menu items appear only once it is added. Settings, Plug-ins has Manage, the Catalogue (sources can be added from a link) and a page for each plug-in. Catalogue versions are checked against their checksums, State shows each plug-in's version and What is new after an update, and Update, Roll back and Update from an address are on Versions. A plug-in that stops answering says Not answering, and one that is idle rests until it is needed.
- **Plug-ins can do more (plug-in API 1.2 to 1.13).** Plug-ins may now restyle the whole of Starboard (up to 32 styles), use a folder inside the media folders they mount, set up before they are activated, hear events and run jobs, lay out their settings in sections, use Reelix's key and your news server when you approve it, draw their own screens with menus, folding tables, filters, links and file uploads, put a VPN app's port on Gluetun for your home network, add files to your media folders as the media folder's owner (never as root) and run whole apps. Plug-ins made for API 1.0 and 1.1 work as before.
- **Discover by area.** Discover has a page each for Films, TV, Music, Radio, Books and Audiobooks, from Reelix's own lists. Settings, General, Discover rows chooses each area's rows and their order, including a row for each genre (such as Comedy or Science fiction) for films, series and radio. Recently added follows what arrived, so new episodes come first, On demand history shows what you started watching, with Add to wanted and Remove, and pictures are kept for four weeks, with Clear the picture cache in Settings, Performance.
- **Discover, Radio.** One search across every radio source, with From radio stations and Podcast feeds only; the genres the sources share as chips; every BBC station in a picker by group; and each source's own categories under More categories.
- **Search filters and Search the sites.** Search filters results by whether they are in your library, kind, year or decade, genre, status (Have it, Missing, Downloading, Below the quality wanted), released or coming soon, followed or not, language, rating and radio source, and remembers them for your account. Search the sites is its own page: one search across the sites Reelix searches, to compare size, seeders and age, with Get this one.
- **Complete collections and series.** An opened film collection shows every film in it, and audiobook and book series come from Audiobookshelf with the missing books from Audible, each with Add missing. Books, Series and Authors are banners like audiobooks, covers of anything not in your library yet are faded behind a dashed outline, and Library, Audiobooks, Narrators lists everyone who reads your audiobooks, with a page each.
- **More in the Library.** Books, music and podcasts have their actions: Quality and folder, Select several, Files and history, and Podcasts and Keep. Change match searches for the right title and moves the item in Reelix with its files kept, or puts the old match back. Library, Radio, Stations is a square grid like TV networks.
- **Bring in music, and folders nobody lists.** Library, Music brings in the artist folders already on disk, as films, series, books and audiobooks do, each artist following only the albums you have. A folder with no match, such as a home video, can come in with your own title, year, description and picture once Reelix can take it (Reelix task RFX-90).
- **Downloads and Wanted.** Activity, Downloads lists Held downloads, the releases a delay profile is holding back, with Get it now and Remove; Choose the episode handles files Reelix refused for their episode number; and paused unpacking in NZBGet is said, with Resume unpacking. Wanted has an item for each media type with Missing and Below the quality wanted, and each film or episode has Details with what Reelix last did and See what Reelix can find. History has Keep history for and Delete history.
- **More of Reelix in Settings.** Settings, Reelix has Tags, Path mappings and Site list updates; Reelix's health shows a broken yt-dlp or FFmpeg, an expired Spotify sign-in and YouTube downloads that keep failing. Settings, Radio has Your podcast library (files Reelix has not matched, with Match by hand), Add a show from a link, and a Catch-up downloads switch for each source that has catch-up.
- **Live TV, your own channel lists.** Live TV takes your own M3U or JSON channel lists and XMLTV guides, by address or as a file up to 60 MB, each with Activate, Deactivate, Test and Remove, and adds them to Jellyfin as one more tuner. Channels fold into sections by list, region and section, each with its own switch and a table to sort, filter and tick several; a list longer than 2,000 channels says only the first 2,000 are read. The dashboard can show On TV now, and logos stay legible.
- **Dispatcharr from Starboard.** Settings, Apps, Dispatcharr sets up the Dispatcharr add-on without opening it: its admin and a key, your IPTV playlist or Xtream Codes server and guide, the channels, and Dispatcharr in Jellyfin as a tuner with its guide.
- **Tdarr from Starboard.** Starboard gives Tdarr its key, and its settings have the basic choices: which libraries, save space or check only, how many at once, and any time or in quiet hours. With the Tdarr plug-in, films and series are converted in a folder on the media disks, so working copies never fill the system disk.
- **Recommended Jellyfin plug-ins.** Settings, Apps, Jellyfin offers skipping intros and credits (from downloaded times, or analysed here in quiet hours only), Playback Reporting, Merge Versions and Trakt, each installed and set up through Jellyfin and shown with its version. Choosing another deactivates the old one; nothing is uninstalled.
- **Kavita in Install apps.** Kavita, the ebook reader, is in the Ebooks group. It reads the media folder only and has a Still to set up step.
- **Full backups without media.** System, Backup and restore makes a full backup of Starboard, its plug-ins, the set-up files and every app's settings and database, encrypted with a password you choose, on another disk, keeping the newest 5; it says which disk and warns when the folder is on the system disk. When backups are made can be Manual, Weekly or Nightly, and Copies off this computer sends each backup to another disk, a network share or your own rclone remote. A backup restores onto a fresh install in both set-ups.
- **Clean up.** System, Clean up lists what is left over (removed plug-ins, an unfinished backup's work folder, older update copies) with sizes and moves what you choose to the holding folder, where each thing can be put back, or deleted for good after you confirm with your password. Settings, Updates, Restore keeps the copies of the last 5, 10 or 20 versions.
- **After an unexpected shutdown.** After a power cut or a crash, Starboard checks once the folders it watches, the pool, any pool job or full backup that stopped part way, and the apps, then says what it found on System, Overview and through Alerts.
- **Server care from the terminal.** ./settings.sh changes settings through Starboard with your password, starting with automatic updates, and sudo deploy/headless.sh readies a computer to run with no screen (never sleeping, the lid ignored, starting again after a crash), showing each change first and with --undo to put them back.
- **Where you are and your keyboard first.** The Run first script and both set-ups ask where the computer is and which keyboard it has, as lists you move through with the arrow keys, then set the time zone, keyboard layout and language; the apps take the time zone. The keyboard step says where symbols such as |, \ and # really are on UK, Irish, German, French, Spanish and Italian keyboards.
- **Audio description in the first set-up.** Both set-ups' Jellyfin extras step offers Audio description, unticked. Ticking it makes Settings, Plug-ins offer the Audio description plug-ins from the Catalogue; Not now forgets it.
- **Own style colours and design pictures.** Starboard's own style has six colours to choose from: Teal (the standard), Indigo, Forest, Plum, Ember and Graphite; Reset style comes back to them, and the own style comes first in the Styles gallery. Default design and Display's Design list show a small picture of each design.
- **Moving in from other apps, step by step.** Move them into Reelix, Take over my apps, Roll back and Clear away open a dialog with each step and its result, and carry on if it is closed. Install apps can do the move too, more site keys are read for you, and after the move you can review the held sites, add titles that did not match (they wait with their IDs and are added once they match), choose Reelix's own requests or keep Seerr, and clear away the apps Reelix replaced into a holding folder.
- **Restart in Manage apps.** Each app's menu has Restart. Restarting Gluetun also restarts the apps that use its network.
- **Comparisons in the help.** A new help section compares Starboard's stack with other media server stacks, with the memory and processor measured at rest, how the tests were run and how to repeat them.

## Changed

- **Settings pages on the second menu.** The parts of Settings, General (now Names and apps, Appearance, Pages and lists, Download hours, and Logs and feedback), Downloads (VPN, Usenet, Download hours), Radio, Performance and Security are each a page of their own on the second menu, and each page asks only for what it shows. Style is now Appearance, with the accessibility options. Old links still work.
- **Settings that work the same everywhere.** Every folder is chosen with the location picker; switches and choices apply at once with a saved note beside them instead of a pop-up message; typed text has one Save per section; lists such as Reelix's sites and API keys are rows that open; and danger zones come last.
- **The same item for the same job everywhere.** Across Apps, the Dashboard, the Library, Discover, Coming up, Activity, System and Install apps: app cards show at most two chips and one button, filters that pick one of several are segmented controls, a state sits on its title's line, lists open in slide-outs instead of dialogs, and one primary button is in view. Apps, Updates updates the apps you tick, and Still to set up has one button and shows only the part each step needs.
- **Both set-ups ask the same questions.** The browser and terminal set-ups ask the same things in the same order, with the same words. Let me choose starts from the recommended apps (Basics), films are Films, not movies, and Reelix's set-up code is read for you so you never see a second code. The set-ups now warn when the apps ticked need more memory than the computer has, leave out apps that do not run on a Raspberry Pi, and stop on a 32-bit system or with under 10 GB free.
- **Lighter on memory and processor.** Heavy jobs (the watch history import, listening progress, Live TV and building Library lists) run in a short-lived process of their own, so the server stays small: building a 10,000-film list now leaves it at 35 MB. The hourly watch history import reads the whole library only when something changed (a run with nothing new from 1.7 seconds to 0.01), the Library keeps a list it has just read for 2 minutes (a search of 10,000 films from 172 ms to 13 ms), plug-in files are read once while unchanged, and an idle Live TV plug-in wakes 12 times a minute instead of 120.
- **Load more as you scroll.** Library walls, Wanted and History load the next page as you scroll, say how many more are shown, leave focus where it was, and pause after three pages so the footer stays in reach; Show 40 more always works.
- **Footer, release notes and loading.** The footer says Upgrade available when a newer release is out, and its Release notes link says it opens a slide-out; Sign in and Set up have no footer. A thin line under the menu shows a page is loading, and Settings pages use the full width.
- **Network logos in their own colours.** Network logos keep their own colours, on a dark or light tile chosen by their tone.
- **Seerr, Overseerr and Jellyseerr.** Seerr is recognised wherever Overseerr or Jellyseerr are, including by its image when taking over your apps, and has its own icon.
- **Memory figures in the help.** The help and Install apps give Reelix's and Jellyfin's memory at rest and while working, measured on a large library, so each install's range is wider and more honest.
- **The plug-ins collection needs this release.** Most plug-ins in TheMightyTRex/starboard-plugins now ask for plug-in API 1.8 to 1.13, which this release brings; Starboard 0.16.5 and earlier had plug-in API 1.1.

## Removed

- **Note an issue.** The old Note an issue tester has gone; Send feedback replaced it. Notes it saved are still listed in Settings, General, Logs and feedback.
- **Storyarr.** Storyarr, retired on 11 November 2026, is no longer offered by Install apps or the set-ups. A server that already runs it keeps it and still updates.

## Fixed

- **An update could stop at its backup.** On a server with plug-ins, ./update.sh stopped before updating anything, because each plug-in's own data folder belongs to the plug-in's user and could not be read for the backup. The backup now leaves those folders out (the plug-ins' settings are still in it), and going back after a failed start keeps them with their plug-in. Updates from this version on use the fixed script.
- **Bringing in books, audiobooks and radio.** Bringing in audiobooks and books sends the quality profile Reelix needs, and their matches read well in the list; radio shows match only when the titles agree, with English stations first.
- **Issues reported on GitHub.** Download states such as post_processing show as words (#7); a plug-in's Activate sits under its state (#8); Sign out is in the side menu (#9); Manage's rows and the Advanced settings switch line up (#10, #11, DASH-413); two-tone logos are legible (#12, #13); an opened collection takes the page heading with Add missing on its line (#14, #15); and Discover's buttons line up, its rows with arrows and fades (#16, #17).
- **Apps on the VPN after Gluetun restarts.** Apps on Gluetun's network rejoin it after Gluetun restarts or is made again, and the VPN apps check runs every 5 minutes whether or not Alerts is active.
- **Accessibility.** 48 findings from the accessibility check are fixed, and a screen reader keeps its place: a page drawn again never sends focus to the top, whole lists are no longer read out, form slide-outs start in their first field and Show more moves to the first new row.
- **Backups and moves that fail.** A full backup that fails sends the backup alert and leaves no part copy behind. When qBittorrent stops accepting sign-ins after wrong passwords, the move says so, for how long and how to clear it, and the steps after a stopped move say they did not start.
- **Plug-in pages.** A saved setting reaches a running plug-in at once, a button without an ID works, no stray null shows on pages with no introduction, and the Catalogue no longer scrolls sideways on a phone.
- **API checks.** The API description, its reference, the contract tests and every example plug-in are checked in one command on every push; the description had fallen behind the routes.

## Security

- **Folders checked where they are used.** The folder picker follows links and refuses a folder outside the usual places (a link under /mnt to /etc is refused), and a plug-in's folder must sit directly in Starboard's plug-ins folder.
- **Passwords kept out of logs.** The set-up password is hidden from every log line, even when an app repeats it in an error message.
- **Exact addresses for YouTube and Spotify.** A link counts as YouTube or Spotify only when its host is exactly theirs, so a link that mentions spotify.com in its path is not taken as Spotify.
- **No slow checks on crafted input.** The TMDB key check and the check of a plug-in's settings values (code scanning #53) no longer slow down on long crafted input: a 210 KB value is checked at once.
- **TLS 1.2 for news servers.** Connections to a news server over SSL need TLS 1.2 or newer.
- **Plug-ins see only what you approve.** A plug-in's key reads only what you approved when you added it, catalogue versions are checked against their checksums, and a plug-in that adds files to your media runs as the media folder's owner, never as root.
- **API keys that cannot change your server.** No API key can change settings, keys, plug-ins or the install; each change made with a key is in the audit log; and the log a key can read has addresses, names and keys taken out.
- **Backups and keys stay private.** Full backups are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a password Starboard never stores; a share's password and an rclone set-up stay in Starboard's own folder, never in a backup or sent to the page; site keys typed for a move are never stored or logged; and the rclone set-up handed to a copy is readable by Starboard alone (found by this release's scan).
- **Checks.** The unit tests (also as another user), the browser journeys, the accessibility checks, the API contract tests and example plug-ins, the help check, the private details check and the full security scans, with CodeQL, ran on this release.

## Your settings and the way back

Settings, General, Downloads, Radio, Performance and Security have their parts on pages of their own; old links still work. Starboard now keeps the saved settings copies, and update.sh's data copies, of the last 5 versions and removes older ones by itself (choose 5, 10 or 20 in Settings, Updates, Restore). Backups are not scheduled until you choose. Tdarr's Overnight is now In quiet hours. The upgrade guide offers Audio description in Jellyfin's extras. To go back, run ./update.sh --to v0.16.5.
