# Starboard v0.7.6

Released 4 November 2026.

## Added

- **A menu in three levels.** A side menu beside every page has the Starboard logo, Search and five sections: Library, Discover, Activity, System and Settings. The level 1 menu along the header holds a section's pages, and the level 2 menu under it holds a page's own tabs. Every page keeps its address.
- **Server name.** Give your server a name of up to 10 characters in Settings, General. It shows in bold at the start of the level 1 menu. Phones show only the logo.
- **Help for each section.** A Help button beside Display opens the help for the section you are in, in a new tab.
- **Places for Reelix.** The Library (TV, Films, Radio, Books and Audiobooks), Trending, Recently finished, Wanted, History and Settings, Reelix have their places in the menu. Each says it comes with Reelix.

## Changed

- **A new logo.** The Screen logo, a screen with four tiles and a star, in the side menu and the header. The browser tab and the app icon keep the old one for now.
- **Where things are.** Some pages moved in the menu. For example, Jellyfin and Audiobookshelf are under Settings, Apps; Updates and Other services are under System, Apps; Downloads and Requests are under Activity. The help says where each one is.

## Removed

- **Shelf.** The Shelf design no longer adds its own icon rail, because every design now has the side menu.

## Security

- **Checks.** Trivy, OWASP ZAP (signed out, and an active scan signed in), Gitleaks, Bandit, ShellCheck, Hadolint and CodeQL ran on this release. They found nothing to fix; ZAP's two warnings were false alarms, explained in docs/SECURITY-AND-TESTS.md.

## Your settings and the way back

Nothing to choose. A dashboard name of 10 characters or fewer becomes the server name, which you can change or clear in Settings, General. To go back, run ./update.sh --to v0.7.5.
