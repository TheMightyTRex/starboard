# Starboard v0.13.2

Released 9 November 2026.

## Added

- **Update in the side menu.** The version is now at the bottom of the side menu, and when a newer Starboard is out, Update with a dot shows above it, linked to About and updates; the footer's About and updates says (1) too. Help: [Update Starboard](https://github.com/TheMightyTRex/starboard/blob/main/docs/help/topics/update-starboard.md#find-out-whether-an-update-is-out).

## Changed

- **The footer.** The footer is the design system's: Starboard's name and logo, then Help, Release notes, GitHub repository and About and updates, with the TheMightyTRexUK mark on the right.
- **Reelix's updates come from your fork.** Apps, Updates checks the Lite or full image Starboard installed, from TheMightyTRex's Reelix, and its What is new reads that branch's changelog; Reelix's own check of upstream's releases is no longer shown. Help: [Update your apps](https://github.com/TheMightyTRex/starboard/blob/main/docs/help/topics/update-your-apps.md#what-is-new-in-reelix).

## Fixed

- **Reelix's logo.** Reelix's logo now shows on its card on servers where Starboard had looked for an icon before 0.13.1 and found none.

## Security

- **Checks.** The unit tests (486), the browser journeys (67), the help check, the private details check and the security scans ran on this release.

## Your settings and the way back

Nothing changes in your apps. To go back, run ./update.sh --to v0.13.1.
