Restore

Put back settings Starboard saved, or go back to an earlier version.

Restore your settings

Starboard saves a copy before every update and every restore. Restoring puts back your apps, categories, design and alerts; your library, downloads, password and sign-ins stay as they are.

      Restore from a file

      A file you downloaded from Settings, General, Backup/Logs.

      Go back to an earlier version

      If a new version does not work for you, run this on the server, in Starboard's folder. It backs up your settings first.

      ./update.sh --to v0.9.0
