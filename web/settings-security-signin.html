Sign-in methods

A two-step code, single sign-on with your own provider, and whether the password works. All are off until you set them up.

For the other people who use your apps, the Single sign-on plug-in is recommended: each person gets their own sign-in, and you keep the list in Settings, People. Leave it off on a server only you use. You choose its Tailscale name once, because changing it later makes every passkey stop working. Add it in Settings, Plug-ins, Catalogue.
