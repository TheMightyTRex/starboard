Starboard

Sign in to Starboard

Forgotten the password? On the server, run ./reset-password.sh in the Starboard folder. The help centre explains it, under Reset a forgotten password.

Or

Sign in with single sign-on

Password sign-in is deactivated. If single sign-on does not work, run ./reset-sign-in.sh on the server, in the Starboard folder. The help centre explains it, under Get back in when sign-in is locked.

Enter your two-step code

6 digits. If you cannot get to the app, enter one of your recovery codes instead.

Lost the app and the recovery codes? On the server, run ./reset-sign-in.sh in the Starboard folder. The help centre explains it, under Get back in when sign-in is locked.
